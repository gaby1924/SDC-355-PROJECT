ABOUT ME

I'm Gaby, a full-time student at ECPI, full-time boy-mom, and part-time coffee addict. I'm deeply passionate about combining software engineering and UX/UI design to create thoughtful, user-centered work. I've always been fascinated by technology and how it can be used to solve real problems and improve people's lives. My journey began as a Hair Stylist, but I quickly realized that my true passion lay in the world of technology. After many years of self-doubt, I finally developed the courage to pursue my dream of becoming a software developer and UX/UI designer. I enrolled in ECPI University, where I'm currently studying Software Development with a focus on UX/UI Design. My goal is to become a unicorn, a jack-of-all trades in the tech world if you will. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my son and writing. I'm actually in the process of writing several books: a devotional, a young adults novel, and a book of poetry.
