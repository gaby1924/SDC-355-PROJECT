Goodreads Redesign

This project was a redesign of the Goodreads website, which I completed as part of my Interface Design Course. The purpose of this project was to select a target company, identify a business problem, propose a solution, and incorporate this through my designs. Throughout this project, I conducted user research, created wireframes and prototypes through an extensive iterative design process, and produced a final high-fidelity prototype that addressed the pain points of the original website and business goals. The business problem statement I developed: The current purchase flow introduces friction by redirecting users away from the platform instead of enabling seamless on-platform book purchasing through Amazon Prime integration (and other integrations). This has created a long-term revenue gap for Amazon as a whole. By implementing on-platform book purchasing through Amazon Prime (Kindle, Audible, etc.), and by redesigning the current outdated website to match the necessary modern upgrades, Amazon will experience an increase in revenue and an increase in user traffic. New Goodreaders will feel more inclined to revisit and repurchase from Goodreads, and veteran Goodreaders will appreciate the improvements and remain loyal.
